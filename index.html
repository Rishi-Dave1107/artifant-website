Who We Are

A specialist IT staffing partner for regulated, high-stakes industries

Artifant Technologies is a US-based IT consulting and staffing firm headquartered in Grand Island, Nebraska. We help enterprises build stronger technology teams by placing professionals who bring both technical depth and an understanding of the business they support.

Our recruiters focus on the roles that drive transformation: analysis, agile delivery, engineering, Salesforce, data governance and program leadership.
